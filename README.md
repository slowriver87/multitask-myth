# Multitask Myth

A 60-second phone game that shows the hidden cost of switching between two simple tasks.

1. Tap 1 through 12.
2. Spell MULTITASKING.
3. Do both, alternating: 1, M, 2, U, 3, L ... 12, G.

Round 3 is the same 24 taps as rounds 1 and 2 combined. The extra time is your switching cost.

## What it measures

- **Extra time** for round 3 versus rounds 1 and 2 added together.
- **Cost per switch**: the extra time spread over the 23 switches.
- **Slowdown back to numbers vs into letters**: research predicts switching back to the stronger habit (counting) costs more.
- **Wrong-sequence taps**: tapping the next item from the other sequence, a sign of task-set inertia.
- **History** of your last 20 runs, saved in your browser only.

## Limits

One run is noisy. Round 3 mixes the cost of switching with the cost of tracking two places at once. Rounds 1 and 2 give practice, which makes round 3 look better than it would cold.

## Background

Allport, Styles and Hsieh (1994); Rogers and Monsell (1995); the clinical Trail Making Test.
